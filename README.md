### 🔎 About me

I'm a Computer Engineer with over 6 years of experience in the Information Security field. I currently work as a **Senior Information Security Architect**, leading a team of security analysts focused on mapping application threats and risks and architecting solutions that are as secure, compliant, and resilient as possible. I have hands-on experience in **Blue Team, AppSec, and DevSecOps**.

Alongside that, I'm an **MSc student in Computer Science at PPGC/UFRGS**, in the research line of **Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence**.

### 🧪 Current research

My master's thesis investigates the feasibility of embedding **Machine Learning models (TinyML)** together with **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) algorithms** directly on low-cost microcontrollers, enabling:

- 🛰️ Real-time detection and reaction to cyberattacks, at the network edge;
- 🔐 Resistance to quantum-computer attacks in **residential IoT** networks;
- ⚙️ Distributed Artificial Intelligence (DAI) architectures applied to resource-constrained embedded systems.

### 🛡️ Some professional experiences
